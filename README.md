# Student Lifestyle Analysis
Data analysis of student lifestyle factors and their relationship with NEET mock-test performance using Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze the relationship between self-study hours and mock scores
- Study the impact of sleep on performance
- Analyze mock-test frequency
- Compare performance across classes
- Analyze phone usage and study habits

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Jupyter notebook
- pgadmin4
- Power BI
- Microsoft Excel

#### 📊 Key Insights

- **Class Performance:** 11th-grade students achieved an average mock score of **72.6%**, compared with **63.8%** for 12th-grade students.

- **Sleep & Performance:** Students sleeping **6–7.5 hours** had the highest average mock score at **70.8%**, compared with **60.0%** among the students sleeping under 6 hours.

- **Subject Difficulty:** **Physics** was the most commonly reported difficult subject, selected by **54.5%** of students, followed by Chemistry at **31.8%**.

- **Phone Usage:** Students with high phone usage (**more than 3 hours/day**) had an average mock score of **59.0%**, compared with **71.2%** among students using their phones for 1.5 hours or less.

- **Score Trends:** **38.6% of students reported improving scores**, while **18.2% reported declining performance**.

- **Study Time:** The relationship between self-study hours and mock scores was **not strictly linear**. Students in the third study-time quartile achieved the highest average score of **73.2%**, while the highest-study quartile averaged **70.2%**.
